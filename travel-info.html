Travel Information

Practical information to help visitors move around and enjoy Tawau.

✈️ Getting to Tawau

Tawau Airport is the main air gateway for visitors. Arrange airport transport or a rental car before travelling to your destination.

🚕 Transportation

Options include car rental, taxi, e-hailing and local transport. Availability can vary by area and time.

🚨 Emergency

For emergencies in Malaysia, call 999. Save your accommodation and transport contact numbers.

💡 Travel Tips

Respect local communities, keep natural areas clean, check opening hours before visiting and carry enough drinking water for outdoor activities.
